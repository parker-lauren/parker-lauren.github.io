Approach

How I work

The same three rules are built into every tool, and into how I run events.

01

Decide before you buy.

Write down what an event has to produce before signing. A meeting target is an instruction; a fit score is an opinion.

02

Every number has a source.

If it isn't sourced, it's an open question, not an estimate. Honest gaps beat confident guesses.

03

AI drafts, people decide.

The tools gather evidence and do the arithmetic. Judgment, relationships and outcomes stay with the team.
